FastRing: Intermittent Fasting

A fasting timer that never locks the timer. Every plan is free, there is no account, and nothing leaves your phone.

Is the timer really free?

Yes. Every plan, the Live Activity, the Lock Screen widget and the last 7 days of history are free. FastRing Pro adds stage insights, unlimited history and charts, weight tracking with Apple Health, notes and mood, Home Screen widgets and CSV export.

The Dynamic Island stopped showing my fast

iOS ends every Live Activity after 8 hours. Open FastRing and it comes back. The Lock Screen widget keeps counting the whole time.

I stopped eating earlier than I pressed Start

Tap the start time under the ring, or on the idle screen choose "Stopped eating earlier?", and set the real time. The goal moves with it.

How do I cancel or restore Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open FastRing → Settings → Restore purchases.

I don't get notifications

Open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → FastRing, and turn on Allow Notifications.
