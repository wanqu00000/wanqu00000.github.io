FastRing Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

FastRing does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • Your fasts, weights, notes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • Apple Health access is optional, used only for weight, and the data stays on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. FastRing never sees your payment details.
  • Notifications are scheduled locally on your device, only to tell you about your fast.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app.

Questions? See the support page.
